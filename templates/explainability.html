Understanding Explainable AI (SHAP) in Survival Modeling
Cooperative Game Theory & Shapley Values

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) is founded upon axiomatic Shapley values from cooperative game theory. In this context, features act as "players" in a game whose outcome is the model's predicted hazard or survival function. SHAP fairly distributes the credit or penalty for a prediction among all input biomarkers.

Key Mathematical Properties:
  • Efficiency: Sum of feature contributions equals the difference between model output and expected baseline.
  • Symmetry: Identical contributors receive equal importance.
  • Additivity: Ensemble model explanations equal the sum of constituent tree explanations.
Global vs. Local Interpretability

Machine learning in medicine demands both cohort-level governance and bedside individualization:

  • Global
    Population-Wide Importance: Measures the mean absolute impact of each omics and clinical feature across all patients in the training cohort.
  • Local
    Individual Patient Attribution: Explains exactly which biomarkers pushed this specific patient into a high-risk or low-risk category.
