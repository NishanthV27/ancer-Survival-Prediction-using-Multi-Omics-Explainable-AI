What is Multi-Omics Survival Modeling?

Traditional oncological prognosis predominantly relies on tumor staging and histopathology. However, tumors with identical histology exhibit vastly different clinical trajectories. Multi-omics bridges this gap by integrating multiple biological layers:

  • Transcriptomics (mRNA Gene Expression): Quantifies transcript abundance for key oncogenes and tumor suppressors (e.g., Gene A, B, C).
  • Epigenomics (DNA Methylation Beta Values): Captures epigenetic silencing and chromatin remodeling (CpG methylation biomarkers).
  • Clinical Features: Patient baseline age and pathological TNM tumor staging (Stages I – IV).

Explainable AI (XAI) in Oncology

Black-box machine learning models cannot be safely applied in clinical healthcare without full interpretability. Our platform provides dual-level explainability:

  • Global Feature Importance: Uncovers population-wide drivers of mortality hazard across the entire patient cohort.
  • Local Patient Attribution: Explains individual predictions by breaking down how each biomarker increases (hazard) or decreases (protection) patient risk.
Supported Architectures: Cox Proportional Hazards & Random Survival Forests (RSF) with right-censoring handling.
